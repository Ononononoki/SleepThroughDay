## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Adds the Day bed, with a darkwood frame and red straw. Napping in it during the day skips time to nightfall once every player online naps in one.
- The Day bed cannot be used at night, and it tells one of 30 jokes when someone tries. Normal beds that refuse in the morning tell one of 50.
- Bed settings for every bed: sleep with enemies nearby (on by default), without a roof, without a fire, while wet, without moving your spawn point, and in other players' beds.
- Config synced from the server. Only admins can change it in multiplayer.
